**RecuPay Tracker** is an application for organizing recurring payments. It brings subscriptions, their associated payment methods, and upcoming billing dates into one interface so users can understand and manage their ongoing expenses.

## Cross-Platform Product

I developed the main application with **Flutter**, Riverpod, GoRouter, Hive, Supabase, and reusable calendar and chart components. The monorepo also contains an **Astro** marketing site and declarative Supabase schemas and functions.

The architecture supports web and mobile experiences while keeping navigation, local persistence, authentication, and remote data responsibilities clearly separated.

## Result

RecuPay Tracker replaces scattered reminders and statements with a focused timeline of recurring charges, making subscription management easier to understand from a single dashboard.
